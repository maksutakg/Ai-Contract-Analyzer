01 The problem we solve

People sign contracts they don’t fully understand

Today

  • Contracts are long and full of legal wording
  • A lawyer’s review is expensive for individuals and small businesses
  • Unfair terms (cash payment, entry without consent) go unnoticed
  • Finding the related court decisions takes legal expertise
→

With our system

  • Upload a PDF or DOCX, get a plain-language summary
  • Every clause is checked and risky ones are flagged
  • Each risk comes with a concrete recommendation
  • Related Yargıtay, Danıştay and KVKK decisions are attached

Python · rule-based

Gemini API
3

Weighted findings become one score and a risk level.

Python
4
build_legal_queries

Write queries

Each risk is turned into a Turkish legal search query.

Gemini API
5
retrieve_yargi_decisions

Fetch precedents

Decisions are searched per risk, then empty or irrelevant ones are dropped (CRAG).

Yargı MCP + CRAG

Gemini → JSON

Each step is a node in a LangGraph StateGraph; they pass one shared state object (ContractAnalysisState) from step to step.

How the risk score is calculated

15
Low
35

65
High
85
Critical
# each finding
weight = severity_weight × confidence
# whole contract
score = max(average, highest) + density_bonus
density_bonus = min(20, 4 × (findings − 1))
0 · Low25 · Medium50 · High90+

Example · kira_sozlesmesi.docx

Critical Entry without consent85 × conf.
High Rent increase · cash · eviction3 × 65 × conf.
Medium Jurisdiction · deposit2 × 35 × conf.
6 findings → density bonus+20
Final score (capped at 100)100

Taking the highest finding means one critical clause can’t be hidden by many harmless ones.

04 Purpose of the project

Why does this project exist?

To make contract review accessible: anyone should see the risks in a Turkish contract, and the court decisions behind them, before signing.

Understand

A long contract becomes a plain summary, separate clauses and one risk score from 0 to 100.

Prove

Every risk is backed by real Court of Cassation, Council of State and KVKK decisions, not only AI opinion.

Act

Each risk has a recommendation, so the user knows what to question or change before signing.

Not a replacement for a lawyer, but a first, affordable check that anyone can run.
